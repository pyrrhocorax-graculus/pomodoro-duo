# Pomodoro Duo

Pomodoro Duo is a focus timer for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Vision Pro: work sessions and breaks, a
desk mode for the iPhone on its stand, and nothing else to learn. It keeps your settings on the device
and needs no account.

Available on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/pomodoro-duo/id6812808799).
